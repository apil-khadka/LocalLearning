Kolibri brings lessons, practice, and progress offline.

Kolibri is an offline-first learning platform that can serve curated learning materials over a school network. Digital Himalaya runs it as a separate service so we can build on its learner, teacher, and content tools instead of recreating them.

The facility is provisioned and reachable from the portal. No learning channels, classes, or learner accounts have been imported yet.

Learning resources in five formats.

Kolibri organizes resources into channels. The exact subjects, languages, and formats depend on the channel a school chooses to import.

Watch

Videos

Recorded explanations and demonstrations that students can replay at their pace.

Listen

Audio

Spoken lessons and other sound resources available inside an imported channel.

Read

Documents and books

PDF and ePub reading materials, organized into channel topics.

Practice

Exercises

Interactive practice such as selecting an answer, entering a value, or writing a formula.

Explore

Interactive apps

Hands-on learning activities that run as part of a channel resource.

Learning Equality describes its wider Kolibri Library as spanning 173+ languages and topics such as STEM, public health, internet safety, teacher professional development, coding, and life skills. That describes the wider library, not the content currently installed in our prototype. Every school still needs to review each channel for curriculum fit, language, licensing, and storage needs.

Teachers can plan the class and follow progress.

Kolibri includes separate learner, coach, and facility-admin roles. Teachers can organize classes and groups, assign lessons and quizzes, and review learner activity and progress from the coach tools.

  • Classes and groupsOrganize learners and assign coaches.
  • LessonsGive a class or group a curated set of resources.
  • QuizzesCreate assessments and review submitted answers.
  • Progress reportsSee resource activity and lesson or quiz progress.

Choose carefully, then take it offline.

Channels can be imported from Kolibri Studio, another device on the local network, or storage that already contains an exported channel.

  1. 01

    Browse a channel

    Find a public collection or prepare a school-specific channel in Kolibri Studio.

  2. 02

    Review the selection

    Check curriculum, language, rights, file sizes, and the topics learners will see.

  3. 03

    Import a useful subset

    Download selected resources once, or transfer an exported channel to the school server.

  4. 04

    Plan and test the class flow

    Assign a lesson, try it with learner accounts, and verify it with the internet disconnected.

A folder of PDFs in Digital Himalaya does not automatically appear in Kolibri. Custom files need to be prepared as a channel in Kolibri Studio before they can be imported as Kolibri learning resources.

One school server. Two focused tools.

Digital Himalaya currently provides the textbook catalog, its own reader, and the optional tutor interface. Kolibri provides its own content library, classes, lessons, quizzes, and coaching reports.

The portal reports Kolibri's availability and links to its learning interface. Learner accounts and progress are managed inside Kolibri; the two products do not share sign-in or automatically synchronize records.
